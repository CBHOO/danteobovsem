DanteObovsem — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

DanteObovsem is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @danteobovsem. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person.

Data processed: the TikTok access token and open ID of the owner's own account, obtained through TikTok Login Kit, used solely to publish the owner's videos via the Content Posting API. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not transmitted to any third party.

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings, after which stored tokens are deleted.

Contact: direct message to the TikTok account @danteobovsem.
